A LITTLE ABOUT ME

More than just a student.

My journey into software development has not been a straight line. My first experience in a technical working environment was in Ukraine, where I assisted colleagues in a railway technical department. After moving to Denmark, my career took a different direction.

Eight years in Danish workplaces gave me valuable experience in teamwork, responsibility and working with people from different backgrounds. During that time, I also learned Danish, completed Prøve i Dansk 3 (PD3), and became familiar with Danish working culture.

Today, I am studying Software Technology Engineering at VIA University College. I enjoy discovering how software works, solving problems, and learning through practical team projects. I am excited to keep growing as a developer and contribute to a real software team.
